# VoltRide Warranty & Service FAQ (fictional sample document)

## What does the warranty cover?

The scooter frame and motor are covered for 3 years or 40,000 km, whichever comes first.
The battery pack is covered for 5 years or 60,000 km. Under the battery warranty, VoltRide will repair
or replace the pack if its usable capacity drops below 70% of the original capacity within the
warranty period.

## What is not covered?

- Damage caused by accidents, misuse, racing or unauthorised modifications.
- Water damage caused by submerging the scooter beyond its IP rating, or by pressure-washing the
  battery connectors or charging port. Riding through normal rain is fine and remains covered.
- Wear items: tyres, brake pads, belt, mirrors and bulbs.
- Damage caused by using a charger that is not approved by VoltRide.

## How do I make a warranty claim?

1. Open the VoltRide app and go to **Support → Warranty claim**.
2. Upload a photo of the problem and your vehicle identification number (VIN).
3. A service advisor will call you within 2 working days to book an inspection.
4. Bring the scooter, the charger and your purchase invoice to the service centre.

Claims are usually resolved within 7 working days. If a part has to be imported, it can take up to
21 working days; you can request a loaner scooter during this period.

## Is roadside assistance included?

Yes. Free 24x7 roadside assistance is included for the first 2 years. It covers towing up to 50 km
to the nearest authorised service centre and on-site help for flat tyres and battery issues.

## Can I transfer the warranty if I sell the scooter?

Yes. The remaining warranty transfers to the new owner free of charge. Both owners must confirm
the transfer in the VoltRide app within 30 days of the sale.

## Does a battery swap change my warranty?

Swapping in another genuine VoltRide battery does not affect the warranty. Third-party batteries
void the warranty for the battery, controller and charging system.
