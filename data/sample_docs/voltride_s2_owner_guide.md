# VoltRide S2 Electric Scooter — Owner Guide (fictional sample document)

## 1. Specifications

- Motor: 2.2 kW hub motor, peak power 3.0 kW
- Battery: 2.9 kWh removable lithium-ion pack (NMC chemistry), 60 V nominal
- Top speed: 70 km/h in Sport mode, 50 km/h in Eco mode
- Certified range: 110 km in Eco mode; real-world range is typically 80–95 km
- Kerb weight: 98 kg; maximum load (rider + pillion + luggage): 150 kg
- Water resistance: IP67 for the battery pack, IP65 for the motor and controller

## 2. Charging

The standard 650 W portable charger charges the battery from 0 to 100% in about 5 hours 30 minutes,
and from 0 to 80% in about 4 hours. The optional VoltRide FastDock (1.5 kW) charges 0 to 80% in
1 hour 45 minutes. Always charge between 5 °C and 45 °C. If the battery is colder than 5 °C,
the scooter will pre-warm it automatically and charging will start a little later.

For a longer battery life, keep the daily charge between 20% and 80% and charge to 100% only before
long rides. If you store the scooter for more than 30 days, leave the battery at 50–60% and top it up
once a month.

## 3. Riding modes

- **Eco**: limits speed to 50 km/h and softens acceleration for maximum range.
- **Ride**: balanced mode, top speed 60 km/h.
- **Sport**: full power, top speed 70 km/h. Range drops by about 25%.
- **Reverse**: hold the mode button for 2 seconds while stationary; speed is limited to 5 km/h.

Regenerative braking recovers energy when you close the throttle. It is strongest in Eco mode.

## 4. Routine maintenance

| Interval | Task |
|---|---|
| Every 2 weeks | Check tyre pressure: front 29 psi, rear 33 psi |
| Every 3,000 km | Inspect brake pads; replace if thinner than 2 mm |
| Every 6,000 km | Brake fluid check and belt tension adjustment at an authorised service centre |
| Every 12 months | Full service and firmware health check |

Do not use a pressure washer near the charging port, the dashboard or the battery connectors.

## 5. Dashboard error codes

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Throttle signal fault | Switch off, wait 30 seconds, switch on. If it persists, visit a service centre. |
| E03 | Motor over-temperature | Stop and let the scooter cool for 15 minutes. Avoid long climbs in Sport mode. |
| E05 | Battery communication lost | Remove and re-seat the battery pack until it clicks; clean the contacts with a dry cloth. |
| E07 | Battery cell imbalance | Charge to 100% with the standard charger and leave it connected for 2 more hours so the cells can balance. If E07 appears again within a week, book a battery inspection. |
| E09 | Side-stand down | Retract the side-stand; the motor will not engage while it is down. |
| E12 | Firmware update failed | Connect to Wi-Fi and retry the update from the VoltRide app. Do not switch off during updates. |
